Friendify

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

Friendify operates the Friendify mobile app and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, and protected when people use Friendify.

Information We Collect

Friendify may collect account details such as name, phone or email login information, profile photo, bio, language, topics, city, state, country, listener status, availability, wallet balance metadata, and support request details.

Friendify may process chat and call metadata such as participants, timestamps, call status, duration, billing or settlement metadata, reports, moderation actions, and notification tokens. Users should avoid sharing highly sensitive personal information in chats or calls.

Friendify may receive payment and wallet metadata from payment providers, including order identifiers, payment status, amount, currency, timestamps, and settlement or withdrawal records. Users should never send OTPs, full card numbers, UPI PINs, bank passwords, or other payment secrets.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the app, authenticate users, show profiles, connect chats and calls, process wallet and withdrawal activity, send notifications, prevent fraud, investigate abuse, provide support, improve reliability, and comply with legal or safety obligations.

Safety, Reports, and Moderation

Reports, blocks, support requests, and safety-related activity may be reviewed by authorized administrators. Friendify may restrict accounts, remove content, pause wallet activity, or preserve records when needed for user safety, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal compliance.

Sharing

Friendify may share limited information with service providers that help run the app, including Firebase or Google Cloud, payment providers, notification providers, analytics and reliability tools, and support operations. Information may also be shared if required by law, to protect users, to prevent fraud, or to enforce app terms.

Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate Friendify, provide support, resolve disputes, meet accounting or payment obligations, prevent abuse, comply with law, and maintain backups. Some records may remain after account deletion when required for safety, payment, fraud prevention, or legal reasons.

Account Deletion

Users can request deletion from Profile > Delete Account Request, or through the support page. Requests are reviewed before completion. Some records may be retained as described above.

Children

Friendify is not intended for children below the age required by applicable law to use online social or payment services. If a child has used Friendify without proper consent, contact support.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, or account deletion help, use the Friendify support page.
