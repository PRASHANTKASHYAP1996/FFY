Friendify

Support and Account Deletion

Last updated: 21 May 2026

Friendify support is available from the app through Help & Support and Profile > Delete Account Request. During beta testing, support requests are reviewed through the app support workflow.

What To Include

When contacting support, include the issue type, date, approximate time, amount if payment-related, device model if relevant, and a short description. Do not send OTPs, passwords, card numbers, UPI PINs, bank passwords, or private identity documents unless specifically requested through a secure process.

Common Support Topics

  • Account login or profile issues
  • Chat or call issues
  • Wallet credits, payments, refunds, or withdrawal questions
  • Reported users, blocked users, or safety concerns
  • Account deletion requests

Safety and Crisis Support

Friendify is not an emergency service. If a user is in immediate danger, feels unsafe, or may harm themselves or someone else, they should stop using the app and contact local emergency services or crisis support immediately.

Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion from Profile > Delete Account Request in the app. Deletion requests are reviewed before completion.

Some records may be retained when needed for payment records, fraud prevention, abuse investigations, safety reports, legal compliance, dispute handling, operational logs, or backups.

Response Time

Support requests are reviewed as soon as reasonably possible. Payment, safety, and account deletion requests may require additional verification before completion.
