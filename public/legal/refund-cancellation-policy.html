Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to Friendify wallet credits, paid calls, payment orders, listener earnings, and withdrawal requests where supported by the app.

Wallet Credits and Payments

Wallet credits may be used for eligible in-app features such as calls. A call may reserve credits before it starts, and final billing depends on server-side call status, duration, settlement, and safety or fraud checks.

If a payment fails, is duplicated, is incomplete, or does not credit correctly, users should contact support with the amount, date, payment method, and a short description. Users should not send OTPs, full card numbers, UPI PINs, bank passwords, or other payment secrets.

Call Charges

If a call fails to connect, is rejected, times out, or ends unexpectedly, Friendify may release reserved credits or adjust billing according to server-side records. Refunds or adjustments depend on the final call state, payment status, fraud checks, and operational review.

Refund Review

Refunds are not automatic unless required by law or clearly supported by Friendify records. Refund or credit adjustment requests may be approved, rejected, or partially adjusted after review.

Withdrawals

Listener withdrawal requests may be manually reviewed. Withdrawals may be delayed, rejected, cancelled, adjusted, or held when required for payment verification, fraud prevention, account safety, incorrect payout details, policy review, or legal compliance.

Cancellations

Users may cancel eligible pending withdrawal requests from the app where the option is available. Completed payments, settled calls, and processed withdrawals may not be reversible except where required by law or approved by Friendify after review.

Support

For payment, refund, cancellation, wallet, or withdrawal support, use the Friendify support page.
